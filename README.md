Analytical Model for Road Safety Risk Assessment
Overview
This model identifies high-risk road segments using vehicle speed, safe speed limits, accident history, and proximity to vulnerable zones like schools, markets, and residential areas. It uses weighted scoring to generate a Safety Risk Score and classify roads as low, medium, or high risk, supporting planning and targeted safety actions.

Speed Safety Score – Classification System
The Speed Safety Score is a numerical and categorical system used to classify each road segment based on its safety risk.

Scoring Criteria
Speed Deviation
Difference between actual speed and safe speed limit
Higher deviation increases risk score
Vulnerable Zone Proximity
Near schools, hospitals, markets, or residential areas
Presence increases risk
Traffic Exposure
High pedestrian or mixed traffic areas increase risk
Accident Indicators
Historical accident data or proxy indicators (if available)
Classification
0–30: Safe
31–60: Moderate Risk
61–100: High Risk (Speed-Unsafe Segment)
Output
Each road segment is labeled and prioritized for interventions such as:

Speed calming measures
Warning signage
Enforcement zones
Purpose
This model enables quick identification of critical areas requiring immediate road safety interventions and supports data-driven decision-making.
